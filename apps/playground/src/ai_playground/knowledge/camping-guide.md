# Choosing tents and sleeping bags

A three season tent is intended for typical spring, summer and autumn camping. A four season tent provides more structure for wind and snow but is generally heavier and can be less comfortable in hot weather. The season label does not guarantee safety in every weather condition. Check the actual forecast, campsite exposure and your skills before a trip. A tent's packed weight includes more than the fabric; compare the listed total weight when planning what to carry. A two person label can mean a close fit, so account for room needed for people and stored gear.

For sleeping bags, compare comfort ratings rather than assuming a survival or extreme rating will feel comfortable. Choose a comfort rating below the lowest temperature you realistically expect, leaving a sensible margin for your own cold tolerance. A sleeping pad provides insulation from the ground and affects how warm the whole system feels. Keep the bag dry and follow its washing and storage instructions.
