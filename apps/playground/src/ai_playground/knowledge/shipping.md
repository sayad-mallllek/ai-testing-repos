# Shipping and delivery

Trailhead Outfitters ships fictional orders within the contiguous United States. Standard shipping costs $6.99 and is free when the merchandise subtotal is at least $100.00 before tax and shipping. Standard delivery normally takes three to five business days after dispatch. Express shipping costs $14.99 and normally takes one to two business days after dispatch. Warehouse processing takes one to two business days before dispatch. Weekends and public holidays do not count as business days. These are estimates, not guaranteed arrival dates.

An order with status processing has not left the warehouse. Shipped means it has been dispatched. Delivered means the dummy tracking record marks delivery complete. Use the order database tool to check a specific order; this document cannot tell you the status of an individual order. We do not provide international delivery in this fictional dataset. If an address needs changing, contact support before dispatch. The assistant cannot change addresses, book deliveries, issue refunds or place orders because its tools only read data. Weather and carrier interruptions can affect delivery timing. Do not promise a date based only on the normal shipping range.
